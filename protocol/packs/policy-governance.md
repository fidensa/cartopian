# Policy governance practice pack

Use this optional guidance only for the selected policy outcome. It does not change task risk, grant authority, activate judgment guidance, or impose review independently of the governing contract.

## Stakeholder Review

Identify affected stakeholders, decision owners, implementers, and people bearing material consequences. Record which perspectives and objections were considered, unresolved conflicts, and who has authority to accept the resulting tradeoff. Consultation is evidence, not a substitute for decision authority.

## Compliance

Map each material requirement to its controlling authority, applicable scope, interpretation, evidence, and exception path. Distinguish binding requirements from guidance. Record conflicts, jurisdiction or version limits, and the approval needed for any exception.

## Publication

Verify the approved text, authoritative publisher, audience, channel, accessibility, version identity, and change communication. Prevent drafts or superseded language from appearing authoritative. Record the published location and proof that intended recipients can identify the controlling version.

## Effective Date Checks

Confirm approval, publication, effective, transition, review, and sunset dates separately. Check timezone, jurisdiction, dependencies, grandfathering, and superseded rules. Verify the policy is applied only to states and decisions covered by its effective context.

# Research inquiry practice pack

Use this optional guidance only for the selected research outcome. It does not change risk, activate a judgment card, or add review requirements.

## Source Quality

Prefer sources with direct authority over the claim and record identity, authorship, publication or effective context, and relevant scope. Distinguish primary evidence from commentary, and explain why a weaker source is still fit when a stronger one is unavailable. Keep stale, conflicted, or inaccessible authority visible.

## Methodology

State the question, comparison frame, inclusion and exclusion rules, and how evidence was gathered and weighed. Separate observations from interpretations. Record material assumptions, missing data, alternative explanations, and method limits so another reader can reproduce or challenge the reasoning.

## Fact Checking

Trace every decisive factual claim to supporting evidence and verify dates, quantities, definitions, quotations, and version context. Resolve contradictions through an explicit authority rule. Mark claims that remain unverified, the consequence of relying on them, and the next proof or decision needed.

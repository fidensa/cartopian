# Marketing claim practice pack

Use this optional guidance only for the selected marketing outcome. It does not change task risk, require review, or activate other specialist guidance.

## Audience

Name the intended audience, its decision or need, the channel context, and the action the asset asks it to take. Check that language, proof, format, timing, and accessibility fit that audience rather than an assumed generic customer.

## Brand

Check the claim and presentation against the applicable brand position, voice, naming, and visual constraints. Preserve truthful distinctions between established facts, positioning, and aspiration. Record any deliberate exception and its authority.

## Legal Review

Identify claims, endorsements, offers, privacy uses, regulated subjects, rights, or jurisdictions that may need authorized legal or compliance review. Verify substantiation and required disclosures. This pack flags applicable review evidence; selecting it does not itself create or satisfy an approval gate.

## Launch Measurement

Define the launch outcome, baseline, audience or channel segment, observation window, and decision threshold before interpreting results. Record the measurement source, attribution limits, stop conditions, and what follow-up decision each result would support.
